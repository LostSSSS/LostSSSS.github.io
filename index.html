01 — whoami

Security-minded, from the packet up.

I'm a Computer Science undergraduate at Maldives National University with a strong pull toward networking and cybersecurity. My CCNA training gave me a solid grounding in how traffic actually moves — routing, switching, subnetting, and troubleshooting — and I like working close to that layer.

Most of what I build is hands-on: a TCP port scanner that fingerprints services, a facial-recognition system using OpenCV and dlib, and several Java simulations exploring queues, data structures, and game logic. I'm just as happy configuring a lab in Cisco Packet Tracer or a VM in VMware as I am writing code.

I'm looking for a first role or internship in cybersecurity, networking, or software development where I can keep learning and contribute to real, practical work.
