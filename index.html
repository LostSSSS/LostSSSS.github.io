01 - whoami

Security-minded, from the packet up.

I'm a Computer Science graduate of Maldives National University with a strong pull toward networking and cybersecurity. My CCNA training gave me a solid grounding in how traffic actually moves - routing, switching, subnetting, and troubleshooting - and I like working close to that layer.

My final-year project brought that together: a Network Intrusion Detection System with Explainable AI that classifies network flows as benign or malicious with machine learning, then explains every decision in plain English using SHAP and LIME, all streamed to a live React and FastAPI dashboard.

Most of what else I build is hands-on: a TCP port scanner that fingerprints services, a facial-recognition system using OpenCV and dlib, and several Java simulations exploring queues, data structures, and game logic. I'm just as happy configuring a lab in Cisco Packet Tracer or a VM in VMware as I am writing code.

I'm looking for a first role in cybersecurity, networking, or software development where I can keep learning and contribute to real, practical work.
